# Roadmap

Ideas for future iterations, focused on features people would actually use day-to-day.

## Planned / Under Consideration

- **Reading streaks / calendar heatmap** — `js/ui-streak.js` already tracks streak data; a GitHub-style activity grid would make the streak feel more rewarding visually.
- **Search/filter bar for the library grid** — filter by title, author, or tag. Especially useful now that bulk tagging exists.
- **"Continue reading" widget on the dashboard** — surfaces the 1-3 books with `status: reading` and most recent activity, one click into detail view.
